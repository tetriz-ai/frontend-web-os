# Voice: Tidewatch console

Harbour staff read this on a quay, often in rain, so it says what is true now and what to do next.

- Name vessels by name, then IMO number in brackets: "Morning Star (IMO 9321483)".
- Times are local port time, 24-hour, no seconds: "14:05".
- Say what happened, then what to do: "Feed paused at 13:52. Readings may be late; refresh to retry."
- Never "oops", "uh-oh" or exclamation marks. Never blame the person reading.
- Words we use: berth (not slip), draught (not draft), pilot (not guide), harbourmaster.
